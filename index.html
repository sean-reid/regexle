regexle

        How to play

        Ten clues, one grid. Each clue is a regular expression, and the whole row or column it sits beside has to match it. Fill all 25 cells with letters, then check the grid. You get six checks.

        • Right letter in the right cell.
        • The answer has this letter elsewhere in this row or column.
        • Not in this row or column.

        Tap any clue for a plain reading of it. Tap a small grid under the board to bring one of your earlier checks back.

        A new puzzle arrives at midnight, your time.
